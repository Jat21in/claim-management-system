Frequently Asked Questions

What is the claim process?
Submit your medical reports online, our AI system processes them, and you'll receive a decision within 2-3 business days. Cashless claims available at network hospitals.
Can I include my family?
Yes, you can add your spouse, children, and dependent parents to the policy at additional premium.
Is there a free-look period?
Yes, you have 15 days free-look period to review the policy and cancel for a full refund if not satisfied.
How do I renew my policy?
Your policy will auto-renew. You'll receive reminders 30 days before expiry via email and SMS.
